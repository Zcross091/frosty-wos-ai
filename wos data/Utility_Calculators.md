## 4. 🚀 State Transfer Eligibility & Pass Matrix

### Transfer Rules & Requirements
- **Furnace Level**: Level 25 minimum.
- **Requirements**: No active marches, empty infirmary, no alliance membership at moment of jump, 30-day transfer cooldown.
- **Pass Cost**: Ranges from **1 Pass** (F2P / Low Power) up to **85+ Passes** (Top Whale Leaderboard).

### Power to Transfer Pass Scale

| Power Range | Required Transfer Passes |
|:---|:---|
| Under 30M Power | **1 Pass** |
| 30M – 50M Power | **2 Passes** |
| 50M – 75M Power | **3 Passes** |
| 75M – 100M Power | **5 Passes** |
| 100M – 130M Power | **8 Passes** |
| 130M – 170M Power | **12 Passes** |

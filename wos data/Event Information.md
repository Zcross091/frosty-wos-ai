# \# Crazy Joe

• Alliance defense event — Joe sends waves at member cities and your Alliance HQ.

• To play: Furnace Lv. 7+ and you must be in an alliance when the round runs.

• R4 / R5 pick the start time and difficulty in the event window.

• If your city fails twice, Joe stops attacking you for the rest of that run — you can still send reinforcements to others.

• Runs on a fixed server schedule (often about twice per week — open the event banner for your state).

Waves \& timing (\~40 minutes, 20 waves)

| Wave type | Which waves | What happens |

|-----------|-------------|----------------|

| Member cities | Most waves | Hits alliance cities — defend home or reinforce allies |

| HQ boss waves | 10 \& 20 | Joe attacks the Alliance Headquarters — not personal cities |

| Online focus | 7, 14 \& 17 | In many states these waves prefer online members — plan to be active or mark offline in alliance chat |



Waves 10 \& 20 — Alliance HQ

• Highest coordination point of the event · more points when HQ holds.

• On higher difficulty (10+), HQ waves are much stronger — Joe brings heavier mixed troops, including Sharpshooters in the attack. Start HQ reinforcements early, not when the march is already on the map.



While the timer runs

• Use all marches you can spare for alliance defense.

• Coordinate in alliance chat: who is offline (empty city for points) vs who needs cover.



🎯 Troops, Sharpshooters \& HQ defense

Sharpshooters / Marksmen — keep them home

• Do not send Sharpshooters (Marksmen) to allies or to HQ marches.

• They stay in your city garrison. Points and kills in this event come mainly from Infantry + Lancers you send out.



Reinforcing member cities

• Send Infantry first, then Lancers — fill with your strongest infantry/lancer marches.

• Target allies who still have few reinforcements so your troops actually fight.

• Best heroes: keep on your home barricade and lock them so Joe cannot pull them away.



Waves 10 \& 20 — HQ checklist

\~5 minutes before the HQ wave: recall one march if it is tied up far away.

March Infantry-heavy (add Lancers, no Sharpshooters) to the Alliance HQ.

On difficulty 10–20, treat HQ as mandatory — one weak HQ defense can cost the whole alliance bonus.

Pets / buffs: march size, defense, or health if you have them — activate before the wave lands.



After the run

• Heal wounded troops after the event ends so your city garrison is not oversized for the next day.



📈 Difficulty levels (1–21)

• R4/R5 choose the level when starting Joe — your alliance must have cleared the previous tier to unlock the next (see in-game list).

• Higher level = stronger attack waves and more points — including tougher Sharpshooter stacks on HQ and city waves at level 10+.



Picking the right level

| Situation | Suggestion |

|-----------|------------|

| Training / new alliance | Stay low until everyone knows HQ timing |

| Mid power (levels 5–9) | Raise only if most cities survive without double losses |

| Strong roster (10–15) | Full HQ drills on waves 10 \& 20 before bumping again |

| Top alliances (16–21) | Max points — requires strict marches, offline votes, and preset HQ calls |



Double-loss rule

• Two failed defenses on your city → no more waves hit you this run.

• Waves you already cleared still count toward rewards.



🏆 Points \& rewards

How you score

• Defend your city and earn credit for kills / holding during waves.

• Reinforce allies — you gain points when your troops help their defense.

• HQ waves 10 \& 20 — major point swings for the whole alliance when HQ holds.



Reward types (in-game mail)

• Personal score chests · Alliance milestone chests · Defense point tiers — exact items depend on state age and the difficulty you cleared.



Expert bonus (if unlocked on your server)

• Baldur — Bounty Hunter: +50% Crazy Joe points · extra reward chest every 200k points (max 10 per season).



🌱 Beginners

• Be in alliance before start · free all marches · never send Sharpshooters out · ask R4 when to go offline.



🎯 Officers

• Announce HQ countdown for waves 10 \& 20 · pick difficulty the roster can survive 20 waves · post offline list.



⚠️ Do not

• Rely on peace shields — they do not block Joe.

• Teleport during an active wave on you — defense credit can drop to zero for that hit.

• Eligibility: Furnace Level 16+ (all qualified Chiefs on the state can join).

• One registered time slot per Chief — you may change your slot anytime before that slot locks.

• No troop losses in the mine — armies return fully healed; defeated heroes get a cooldown (see Map card).

• Expert Kathy (Gen 3, eligible states) boosts Frostfire efficiency — see your Dawn Academy tab.

Match flow

• Battle length is shown in the event UI (commonly \~30 minutes — confirm the timer when you enter).

• Arrive early: use the pre-battle staging window to scout veins and patrol density near your spawn.

• Gathering-speed and march-speed buffs from the open world do not apply inside Frostfire — City tech, VIP, pets, and in-mine talents still matter (check tooltips).

🗺️ Frostfire Mine – Map, veins \& Smelter

🗺️ Map, veins \& Smelter

Vein zones (read rates on the map)

Mine patrols

Smelter (centre building)

• Opens in the late match phase (timer on the map — often around the final minutes).

• If defenders are too strong, touch and leave rather than feed cooldowns.



After a fight

• Heroes on a defeat cooldown cannot redeploy until it ends — plan swaps before the last outburst window.



⚔️ Frostfire Mine – Gather \& hunt

⚔️ Two playstyles (community playbooks — not official)

Timers below use minutes:seconds after match start (e.g. 08:00 = eight minutes in).



🌿 Gather (hold Level-3 veins)

• Goal: secure Level-3 tiles, hit vein outbursts, trigger in-mine talent bonuses on a steady rhythm.

• Marches: \~four on veins, \~two on patrols / backup (adjust to your slot count).

• Outbursts (common callouts): 08:00 · 09:00 · 10:00 and 18:00 · 19:00 · 20:00 — recall marches and stack outburst tiles first.

• Smelter: at least one short capture for ranking points if you can march in time.



🏃 Hunt (patrols \& outbursts — F2P-friendly)

• Goal: chain nearby mine patrols, snap outbursts, use quick claim → recall on veins for talent procs.

• If the next patrol is far, take a short vein claim instead of idle marching.

• Teleport when paths exceed \~45–60 seconds of travel — density matters more than raw power.



Talent tree

• Paths differ for gather vs hunt — read each node in the Frostfire talent screen before match day.

• Many veterans run Level 3 left for the periodic Orichalcum proc on new vein claims (sync to the in-game clock).



❌ Common mistakes

• Ignoring outburst timers · losing fights on contested veins · skipping Smelter entirely · fighting without a backup march plan.

# \# Mercenary Prestige

⚔️ What is Mercenary Prestige?

Mercenary Prestige is a recurring PvE challenge event in Whiteout Survival. You and your alliance fight Phaethon Mercenaries who have occupied a nearby mining region and enslaved the locals. Gina's Eagles have taken a contract to clear them out – and you're invited to share in the Prestige and Bounty by helping defeat these mercenaries.



The event is split into two main components:



🧍 Personal Challenges – your own series of mercenary waves on the world map

🛡️ Alliance Challenges – powerful Phaethon Captains fought together by the whole alliance



Mercenary Prestige is about single‑march combat and stamina management, but smart alliances also use it to coordinate around alliance bosses and shared rewards.



\---



📚 Event structure at a glance

📆 Cadence: Mercenary Prestige is a recurring event (often roughly monthly). Exact timing can vary by server and patch; check your in‑game event calendar.

🎚️ Two difficulty tiers:

🟦 Champions – entry‑level tier

🟨 Legends – high‑end tier with much better rewards

🔑 Five difficulty levels per tier: Easy, Normal, Hard, Nightmare, Insane

🚫 Difficulty unlocks: You must clear a lower level in a previous event to unlock the next. At minimum you need multiple Mercenary Prestige cycles to unlock up to Legends Insane.



Once the event starts you select a tier (Champions or Legends) and a difficulty level (Easy → Insane). This applies to all waves for the whole event and cannot be changed later.



\---



🧍 Personal Challenges – your own mercenary waves

Personal Challenges are the core: you fight a sequence of mercenary targets on the world map. Each target is a wave at your chosen difficulty. You must scout each mercenary before attacking. Defeating waves grants Resources, Hero XP, Speedups, Gems, Mythic General Hero Shards, and at higher levels Chief Gear Materials and Fire Crystals.



\---



🛡️ Alliance Challenges – Phaethon Captains

As your alliance progresses, R4/R5 can summon Phaethon Captains. These are alliance‑level bosses on the world map. Any member can send individual marches to chip away at the Captain's shared HP. When defeated, all participants receive extra rewards. Each Captain has a time limit (e.g. 30 minutes) and can usually be defeated only once per event.



\---



🎁 Why Mercenary Prestige matters (for every player type)

🔝 For whales / heavy spenders: Reliable Mythic General Hero Shards, Chief Gear Material chests, Fire Crystals, Gems. Great sandbox to test max marches and hero synergies.

📈 For mid‑spenders: Strong recurring income of Hero XP, Speedups, Gems, Mythic Shards. Progression path from Champions into Legends.

🌱 For F2P / low spenders: Steady drip of Hero XP, Speedups, Resources, and access to Mythic Shards and Fire Crystals over time. With smart difficulty choice and stamina management, excellent value per resource.

🤝 For alliance leadership: Alliance Challenges (Captains) are a coordination test – when to summon, how to ping members, how to turn Personal progress into group‑level rewards. A regular event anchor for alliance activity.



\---



🚩 Part 1 – Key takeaways

Mercenary Prestige = Personal Challenges (your waves) + Alliance Challenges (Captains).

Difficulty = Tier (Champions / Legends) + Level (Easy → Insane). Higher difficulties must be unlocked over multiple events.

Rewards scale strongly with difficulty, especially Legends Nightmare / Insane.

Every player type can get real value – pick the right difficulty and play style.



⚙️ Personal Challenges \& stamina economy

🔍 Scouting mercenaries

To spawn a mercenary target:



Go to Event → Mercenary Prestige and tap Scout.

The game spends 15 Stamina and locates a Phaethon Mercenary on the world map.

You are taken to the world view; you have 1 hour to defeat this target.



If you do not defeat the mercenary in time, the target despawns and you must pay 15 Stamina again to re‑scout.



⚔️ Attack options \& stamina costs

For each scouted mercenary you have three options:



🧍 Solo attack – Default 10 Stamina (with Gina 4★: 8 Stamina). Choose 3 heroes, set troops, send one march.

🚩 Rally attack – Default 25 Stamina (with Gina 4★: 20 Stamina). You start a rally; alliance members who join get extra rewards.

🤝 Request alliance help – You use one Mercenary Contract. Your alliance sees a notification and the target location; someone else can start a rally on your mercenary; rally joiners get additional rewards.



You have a limited number of Mercenary Contracts per event (often 5 base + 1 per time you help someone). Use them strategically.



\---



🔢 Total stamina requirements

All waves via rally: \~1,250–2,000 Stamina (\~1,150–1,750 with Gina 4★).

All waves solo: Lower total stamina, but no rally‑bonus rewards.



Most players mix solo (for easy waves or off‑peak) and rally (when the alliance is active and you want extra rewards).



\---



🧬 Difficulty choice \& progression path

F2P / low‑spenders: Start Champions Easy or Normal. Move up only when you clear the previous level comfortably.

Mid‑spenders: Aim for Champions Hard → Nightmare, then Legends Easy → Normal/Hard as your account grows.

Whales: Target Legends Nightmare / Insane. Mercenary Prestige becomes a main source of Gems, Speedups, Mythic Shards, Chief Gear Materials, Fire Crystals.



Choosing a difficulty that is too high and failing waves wastes Stamina and event value. Better to clear a lower difficulty cleanly than struggle on a higher one.



\---



🛡️ Alliance Challenges – how to handle Captains

When Captains appear: As the alliance progresses, R4/R5 can summon Phaethon Captains. All members get mail; the Captain appears on the map with a timer (e.g. 30 minutes).

Fighting: Any member sends individual marches to chip away at the Captain's shared HP. All who participate at least once get Alliance Challenge rewards.

Important: Each Captain can usually be defeated only once per event. If the timer runs out, re‑activation may be possible (check in‑game).



\---



🎯 Alliance strategy guidelines

🕒 Time Captains for when many players are online and can guarantee enough hits in the timer.

📣 Communicate spawns and a short plan (who hits, how many marches) via alliance chat / Discord.

🤝 Encourage everyone (F2P to whales) to hit Captains at least once for extra rewards.

💡 Combine Captain summons with times when members are already doing Personal Challenges to maximize value.



🎯 Key tips \& advanced play

Stamina management

⏱️ Start early in the event – The earlier you begin, the more natural stamina regen you can use.

🔁 Stagger your sessions – Split play into multiple shorter sessions instead of burning all stamina in one go; reduces risk of timer‑fails on scouted mercenaries and fits alliance activity windows.

🧊 Use Gina (4★) – Lower stamina cost per hit across many waves adds up to large savings. Gina is one of the best stamina‑efficiency heroes for Mercenary Prestige.



March \& hero setup

🧠 Use your best PvE march – Strongest PvE heroes, top DPS, march buffs, sustain if needed. Not experimental line‑ups.

🔄 Adjust if you hit a wall – If you start failing waves, check gear and hero skills, hero order, and troop balance.



Alliance coordination

📌 Fixed „Merc windows“ – Agree on times (e.g. after reset, prime time) when more players scout mercs, R4/R5 summon Captains, and rally leaders start merc rallies.

🧾 Help‑request etiquette – Don't spam help at 3am if nobody is online. Use requests during active windows so you get real help and helpers get wave/rally rewards.



\---



❌ Frequent mistakes to avoid

⛔ Scouting when you can't play – Wastes 15 Stamina and a wave if the 1‑hour timer expires.

⛔ Over‑pushing difficulty – Choosing a level you barely survive increases fail risk and makes each wave more expensive in practice.

⛔ Ignoring alliance Captains – Missing them means leaving „free“ rewards and weaker alliance performance.



\---



✅ Final summary – How to get the most out of Mercenary Prestige

Pick the right difficulty for your account: F2P → Champions Easy/Normal (later Hard/Nightmare); Mid → Champions Hard/Nightmare, Legends Easy/Normal; Whales → Legends Hard/Nightmare/Insane.

Manage stamina – Avoid wasted scouts, use Gina's reductions, schedule sessions around event timers.

Support your alliance – Join rallies, respond to help requests when possible, hit Captains at least once for extra rewards.

Treat Mercenary Prestige as a planned growth window – Mythic Shards, Chief Gear Mats, Fire Crystals, Gems \& Speedups all come together here in a predictable, repeatable package.

# \# State Transfer

🚚 State Transfer – Overview

• Cross-state move for your city — the transfer is permanent; you will not return to your old state automatically (Help Center).

• The event is not on a fixed global calendar — check the in-game schedule each season (Help Center).

• Before a session, servers usually run State of Power or Castle Battle so alliances can plan — exact timing is in your state banner.

• You may only transfer to states in the same transfer group shown in the event UI (WOSC announcements match this rule).



Transfer Score (state rating)

• Each state’s Transfer Score is the sum of the top 100 most active Chiefs in that state (in-game / WOSC event text).

• States are classed as Leading States (higher score) or Ordinary States (all others) — caps and invites differ (see Invites card).

📅 Three phases (Help Center)

| Phase | What happens |

|-------|----------------|

| I – Pre-Transfer | The President sets a Transfer Power Cap for entering the state. If there is no President, the Chief who most recently held the presidency handles this. |

| II – Invitational Transfer | Presidents send Ordinary and Special Invites so Chiefs can join early. The Power Cap cannot be changed after this phase starts. |

| III – Free Transfer | Chiefs may transfer freely if they meet the state’s Power Cap. Chiefs above the cap need a Special Invite approved by the President. |



Phase lengths

• Day counts per phase are shown in the event UI for your season — do not rely on old screenshots from other rounds.



WOSC note

• Some announcements label the President role as Transfer Manager during the event — same duties, in-game title may vary.

✅ Can you transfer? (Help Center)

Target state must match

Same hero generation as your current state.

Same Fire Crystal level as your current state.



Character age vs target state

• Your character must not be “older” than the target state beyond a window that depends on target development — minimum 90 days, maximum 180 days (Help Center).



Second transfer

• 25-day cooldown after a successful transfer before you can move again, even with enough passes.



Characters per state

• Up to 4 characters may exist in one state — you can still transfer if two alts are already there (Help Center).



Does not block transfer (Help Center)

• Dismissing troops or unequipping gear does not change your transfer score.

• You may transfer while holding an office, as an offender, or with troops in Infirmary / Enlistment Office.

🎟 Invites \& state caps (Help Center)

Special Invites

• For Chiefs who exceed a state’s Power Cap.

• Each state starts with 3 Special Invites available; max issued at once: 3 (cannot stockpile above cap).

• 1 spent invite is restored on the 1st of each month (00:00 UTC).

• Leading States cannot issue Special Invites.



Ordinary Invites

• For Chiefs who meet the Power Cap.

• Ordinary States: 35 Ordinary Invites + 20 slots during “Transfer Opens” (transfer cap 55 total).

• Leading States: 20 Ordinary Invites + 10 during Transfer Opens (transfer cap 30 total).



Leading vs Ordinary power limits

• Leading States: power limits for incomers can be set within the top three tiers, preset to the lowest tier.



Notifications

• Special Invites send mail; Ordinary Invites do not.

• President invites cannot be withdrawn — use them carefully.

💰 Passes, resources \& pitfalls (Help Center)

Transfer Passes

• Cost scales with your power: 1 to 50 passes per move.

• Packs sell up to 85 passes per month total across offered packs.

• The invited Chief pays their own passes — the President does not pay for them.



Resources \& events

• Resources above Storehouse Protection are lost on transfer.

• You will not receive leaderboard ranking rewards for ongoing events — only progress-based rewards already earned.



Slots full?

• Transfer slots are first come, first served — prepare early or target another state in your group.

# \# Frost Dragon Tyrant

❄️ What is Frostdragon Tyrant?

Frostdragon Tyrant is one of the largest cross‑state alliance PvP events in Whiteout Survival. Several states with similar progress are grouped into a zone, and within that zone the strongest alliances fight on a dedicated battlefield map.



Key points:



🌐 Zone system: Multiple states with similar progress (Fire Crystal, hero generation, chief gear/charms, pet levels, etc.) are matched into a zone. Only alliances from that zone fight each other.

🤝 Alliance‑based selection: All alliances in a state can sign up during Phase 1, but only a limited number of top alliances by alliance power become eligible and can actually participate.

👥 Combatants (up to 60 players): In Phase 2, R4/R5 assign up to 60 combatants per alliance. Only combatants may teleport to the Frostdragon battlefield and fight there; non‑combatants stay in the normal state but can help with healing.

🗺️ Battlefield map:

Border Region – starting and fallback area; cities destroyed in the Dragon Crypt respawn here.

Dragon Crypt – central warzone containing 🏰 Frostdragon Capital and 🗼 4 Spires (Life, Dragonbane, Shield, Blade).

🚩 Rally‑focused gameplay: Almost all key objectives (Capital, Spires) are decided through rallies, not solo hits.

⏱️ Battle window: The battlefield is usually open for about 8 hours, with the Capital being contestable for roughly 6 hours.



Why does the event matter – for big AND small players?



🔝 Top alliances: Prestige (title, skins, very strong buffs) and live‑fire training for large‑scale rally wars and cross‑state diplomacy.

📈 Mid‑tier alliances: Practice rally coordination, teleport setups and Spire control, and earn strong alliance \& mission rewards.

🌱 Smaller / F2P‑heavy alliances: Benefit from mission, alliance and healing buffs; can safely train rally joining, teleport timing and healing management without having to dominate the Capital to get value from the event.

A. Win condition – Occupation Time \& Tyrant

🎯 Core rule: The winner is not decided by classic event points, but by total Capital occupation time.

🏰 Capital occupation: When your alliance wins a rally on the Capital, you take control and the rally leader of that rally starts accumulating occupation time. If another alliance takes the Capital, your timer stops and the new rally leader starts their own timer. At the end of the event, all holding periods for each chief are summed.

👑 Frostdragon Tyrant: The chief with the highest total occupation time becomes Frostdragon Tyrant and receives exclusive rewards (title, skins, strong buffs, Tyrant’s Gift features, etc.).



B. Phases \& flow (short timeline)

Alliance Sign‑up (\~48h, Mon–Tue): R5/R4 register alliances; the system ranks alliances by alliance power and marks the top ones per zone as eligible.

Combatant Dispatch (\~48h, Wed–Thu): R5/R4 assign up to 60 combatants per alliance; only combatants can enter the battlefield, non‑combatants assist with healing.

Eve of Battle / Preparation (\~59h, Fri–Sun): Mission chains (Frostwind / Champions’ Eve) raise an alliance’s Preparation Level, unlocking better battlefield buffs (healing speed, lower costs, sometimes stat buffs).

Battlefield Opens (\~8h, Sun): Combatants teleport into Border Region; after a short prep time, Spires and later the Capital become attackable, leading to intense rally fighting.

Rearmament (\~54h after battle): Participating states receive Healing Cost –30% and Healing Speed +500%, ideal for clearing wounded.



C. Map \& structures

Border Region: Safer entry \& fallback area with lower PvP density; cities destroyed in Dragon Crypt respawn here – good for regrouping, gathering and healing.

Dragon Crypt: Central warzone containing:

🏰 Frostdragon Capital – central objective that decides the Tyrant title.

🗼 4 Spires – Life (Health), Dragonbane (Lethality), Shield (Defense), Blade (Attack).

Spires: Captured and held via rallies, only attackable in certain windows and then protected for a time. While you hold a Spire, its buff applies to your entire alliance, giving you a huge advantage in Capital fights.

Frostdragon Capital: Attacked/defended via rallies (combatants only), often changes hands multiple times. The First Occupancy Reward grants extra rewards to the first alliance that successfully occupies the Capital.



D. Healing \& losses

🏥 Unlimited infirmary on the battlefield: During the event, Frostdragon battles effectively use an infirmary with no cap; troops become wounded instead of dying permanently (within the event context) and can be healed later with resources and speedups.

📉 After leaving the event: You can take your time healing Frostdragon wounded, but if your normal infirmary is full and you keep losing troops outside the event, new wounded cannot be stored and will die permanently.

⚖️ Rearmament: The 54h Rearmament window (–30% cost, +500% speed) is the best time to heal both Frostdragon wounded and any older wounded stacks quickly and cheaply.



E. Currencies \& systems

💠 Dragonmarks: Main event currency, gained from missions, milestones and correct predictions; used in the event shop and Prediction System.

🔷 Scaled Crystals: Additional event currency (missions, dailies, sometimes packs) used for special shop items/chests, depending on the current patch.

🔮 Prediction System: Lets you bet on which alliance/outcome will win; correct predictions award extra Dragonmarks.

🐉 Alliance Crystal Skills / Dragonite: Powerful alliance‑wide skills that R4/R5 can activate during the event using resources like Dragonite; exact effects and costs change over time, so always read the current in‑game tooltips.



🎯 Strategy, Roles \& Summary

A. Alliance roles

🐉 Rally leaders: Strongest accounts (heroes, gear, troop count) that lead rallies on Capital \& Spires; they need clear calls and reliable support.

💂 Reinforcements / joiners: Fill rallies quickly with correct troop types and reinforce Capital \& Spires to keep them strong.

💊 Healers / support: Track infirmary status, resources and speedups, remind core players to heal on time, and coordinate transfers where allowed.



B. Strategy by alliance type

1️⃣ Top / very strong alliances



Appoint 2–4 primary rally leaders (Capital + Spires).

Optionally choose a Tyrant candidate who should lead most Capital rallies.

Spires before Capital: Secure key Spires (especially Attack/Lethality) first, then push the Capital during buff windows.

Clustered positioning: Place combatants in a tight cluster around Capital \& Spires to minimize march times and maximize reinforcement speed.

Plan rally rotation: Rotate leaders (A/B/C) instead of launching random rallies; only run as many rallies as you can actually fill and heal.



2️⃣ Strong but not top‑tier alliances



Set realistic goals (e.g. “Top‑3 Capital time” or “hold 2 Spires for X minutes”).

Avoid trading into every whale stack; focus on fights where you have buffs and numbers that at least give you a chance.

Don’t sacrifice everything on Capital pushes; secure missions and Spires first, and only contest Capital when conditions (buffs, numbers, timing) are in your favour.



3️⃣ Smaller / F2P‑heavy alliances



Focus on missions, smart fights and survival rather than constant front‑line brawling.

Support friendly rallies instead of trying to solo‑dominate the Capital against heavy spenders.

Use the Rearmament phase to fully rebuild – plan to leave the event with a stronger army than when you entered.



C. Common mistakes to avoid

❌ Ignoring Spires: Without Spire buffs you will rarely win close Capital rallies against strong alliances.

❌ Rally‑leader chaos: Too many uncoordinated rallies at once lead to empty rallies, wasted troops and poor results.

❌ No resource/heal buffer: Burning all speedups and resources in small events and having nothing left for Frostdragon severely limits your impact.

❌ Bad teleport positions: Teleporting too far from objectives or directly into enemy clusters causes unnecessary losses and weak maps.



D. TL;DR – Key takeaways

Frostdragon Tyrant is a cross‑state alliance event on a dedicated battlefield (Border Region + Dragon Crypt).

Only top alliances in each zone are eligible; each can send up to 60 combatants.

The winner is the chief with the highest total Capital occupation time (rally leader time), not the one with the highest score.

On the battlefield, the infirmary is effectively unlimited; after the event, the Rearmament phase gives –30% healing cost and +500% healing speed.

Spires grant huge stat buffs (HP/ATK/DEF/Lethality) and are critical for winning Capital fights.

Important systems: Dragonmarks, Scaled Crystals, Prediction, and Alliance Skills/Dragonite.

Success requires clear roles, strong rally coordination, smart teleport \& healing management, and realistic goals based on your alliance’s strength.



# \# Alliance Showdown

❄️ What is Alliance Showdown?

Alliance Showdown is a multi‑day cross‑alliance PvP event in Whiteout Survival. Instead of fighting city‑vs‑city on a separate map, two alliances compete over several days by earning development points in different areas (building, research, troops, heroes, pets, gear, trade, etc.). At the end of each day, the total points of both alliances are compared – the stronger side gains a daily win / victory points. At the end of the event, the alliance with the better overall score wins.



Key facts from official info \& help center:



✅ Participation: Only strong alliances (typically e.g. Top‑20 by alliance power in the state/zone) are eligible for Alliance Showdown.

✅ Join timing: You must be in your alliance before matchmaking starts (usually Sunday 23:00 UTC) for your points to count towards the alliance score.

✅ Daily themes: Each event day has its own focus, such as city power, heroes, companions, gear, trade, full‑scale competition. Only specific actions earn points on that day.

✅ Daily comparison: At the end of each day the event compares alliance points between the two matched alliances. The winner gains one or more victory points for that day (depending on the day’s weight).

✅ Final ranking: After all days are finished, the alliance with more victory points / daily wins is the overall winner and receives significantly better rewards (speedups, resources, Life Essence, hero fragments, gold keys, etc.).



Alliance changes during the event (per official help):



If you leave your alliance during Alliance Showdown, your contribution to the alliance score is lost as long as you are not in that alliance.

Your personal points and daily chests remain on your account.

If you return to the same alliance, your contributions are usually restored to that alliance (always check the current in‑game description for the exact rule in your version).



Why is Alliance Showdown important – for big and small players?



🔝 For whales / heavy spenders: It is a prestige event with strong rewards and a great place to train coordinated alliance play and timing.

📈 For mid‑spenders and active F2P players: You can extract a lot of value from saved resources and speedups, if you use them on the right days instead of random times.

🤝 For alliance leadership: Alliance Showdown is a stress test for your coordination, communication and planning. Good planning beats raw power very often.



A. Event structure \& daily stages

The exact names and details of the days can vary slightly between patches, but all official and community sources describe a very similar pattern:



⏱️ Duration: Alliance Showdown runs for about 6.5–7 days.

📅 Daily themes: Each day focuses on a different type of progression, for example:

Day 1: City Power / Rise of the City – building, research, troop power

Day 2: Hero development – hero levels, skills, stars, fragments

Day 3: Companions / Pets – pet level, skills, equipment

Day 4: Gear enhancement – chief gear, hero gear, upgrades

Day 5: Trade / economic tasks – trade routes, special tasks

Day 6/7: Full‑Scale Competition – almost all actions count (buildings, research, troops, heroes, pets, gear ...)

🎯 Goal of the event: You don’t need to win every single day. You need enough daily wins / victory points overall (e.g. “at least 7 out of 13 points” – value from known guides; always check the current in‑game description for the exact formula).



How daily scoring works (based on official descriptions):



Every action relevant to the day’s theme grants personal points.

Your personal points are added to the alliance total for that day.

At day end, the event compares total points of your alliance vs. the opponent alliance. The side with more points wins the day and receives victory points.

After the last day, the event compares overall victory points and declares an overall winner.

A realistic plan often looks like this (based on high‑level community guides):

Great days for mid‑spenders and F2P, because saved hero/pet materials convert into solid points.

🎯 Key lessons \& summary

Understand the system – not just “more points”

Alliance Showdown is not about random power growth; it is about structured progression on specific days.



Each day has a theme and counts only certain actions.

Your alliance needs enough daily wins / victory points overall, not a single gigantic push on a random day.

Smart timing and coordination can let a slightly weaker alliance beat a stronger but chaotic opponent.



Alliance planning beats solo ego

Without a plan, players will spend whenever they want, wasting a lot of potential.



With a plan:

You define 2–3 focus days where everyone pushes together.

You accept that some days will be low‑effort days to save strength.

This pattern is recommended in many high‑level community guides, because it balances progress, rewards and long‑term account growth.



F2P can contribute a lot – if they time correctly

Free‑to‑play and low‑spend players do not need to match whales in raw power to be valuable.



By saving upgrades, speedups and materials and using them on the right days, F2P players often achieve excellent value per resource.

A coordinated group of F2P and mid‑spenders can decide close days in favor of their alliance.



Communication is everything

Alliance Showdown is won in chat and planning as much as in the city.



Clear calls like “today focus”, “today low”, “final push in 1 hour” are crucial.

Short status updates (who is ahead, by how much) keep everyone aligned.

A few well‑timed pushes coordinated over voice/Discord can flip an entire day.



After the event, your alliance should be stronger

If you play Alliance Showdown correctly, you do not end up “empty”, but stronger than before:



More research done

Stronger gear

Upgraded heroes and pets

Extra rewards from daily chests and final rankings



Use Alliance Showdown as a structured growth window: plan together, focus on the right days, protect your resources on low‑value days – and your alliance will steadily get stronger while still competing for top rewards.

Rally Attack Procedure 🎯

• Assemble one tile behind the target building (never on top).

• Use two staggered rally attacks (second starts 15–30 seconds after the first).

• Fill with strongest marches first, then the rest.

• Follow with one or two small rallies to keep pressure.

• Use Advanced Teleports deliberately: rally leaders and strongest fighters jump; runners jump only if it shortens the route significantly.

Holding and Defense 🛡️

• Do not let garrison strength fall below about 70%; rotate fresh defenders.

• Keep one or two fast accounts ready to reinforce instantly.

• Announce unified healing windows (for example 90 seconds): two teams heal while two teams hold and fill.

• If a building falls, collect the scattered temporary Arsenal Points immediately.

Collecting Rules (Phase Three) 🏃

• Presets: “Runner One” = maximum march speed; “Runner Two” = safe pickup near the front line.

• Pre-mark three to four safe routes per team anchor.

• Send two to three runners per Weapon Workshop plus one screen to deter disruption.

• While one team collects, the others open short, coordinated fights to create space.

If You Are Behind / If You Are Ahead 📈

• Behind: Re-secure Central Transit Station and Boiler Room to regain tempo; run small repeated rallies on enemy point buildings; temporarily increase the percentage of runners.



• Ahead: Do not overextend; hold the key buildings and prioritize collection; layer defenses on Imperial Foundry and Prototype Sites.

Preparation Checklist 🧰

• Activate attack, march, and relevant event boosts

🏛️ Hall of Heroes – Part 1: Overview

What is Hall of Heroes?



Hall of Heroes (HoH) is a weekly event from Sunday to Tuesday in Whiteout Survival. You spend Marks of Valor to buy generation‑based rewards – including Hero Shards, Mythic General Hero Shards, and Exclusive Gear Widget Chests from older generations.



When does HoH run?



Duration: 3 days (Sunday → Tuesday)

Frequency: weekly

The shop contents and available heroes depend on your server’s current hero generation.



How do you get Marks of Valor?



Gems: 100 free Gems per day; 1 free Mark per event; additional Marks for 150 Gems each (up to 100 Marks per event).

Packs: Once‑per‑day packs with real money (for example $5 / $10 / $20 / $50 / $100).

The daily 100 free Gems and once‑per‑day packs reset every day; the rest of the shop stays for the whole event.



Core idea



HoH is not a general shop, but a generation‑based event. Which heroes and widgets you see depends on your server’s generation. Each generation has exactly one exclusive HoH hero that you can reliably build here.



Quick summary



HoH = Sunday → Tuesday; you spend Marks of Valor for generation‑bound rewards.

Marks come from Gems (150 each, max 100 per event for 13,850 Gems) or from paid packs.

Contents and prices always depend on your current hero generation.

# \# Wild Brawl

🏹 Wild Brawl – Part 1: Overview

✅ TL;DR (30 seconds)

🎯 Goal: Score the most Strategic Points in \~10 minutes

🏛️ Best spike: first full capture of center (Citadel)

🗺️ Stable points: side forts + 🚚 trucks + 🪂 airdrops

🧭 1) What is Wild Brawl?

Wild Brawl is a 10‑player PvP brawl mode in Whiteout Survival. You teleport into a small map with 10 chiefs and fight for control of buildings and objectives. The goal is to earn as many Strategic Points as possible in about 10 minutes.



⏱️ 2) Event timing \& modes

📅 Duration: 2 days

🪟 Match windows: 4 × 2‑hour windows per day

👥 Players per match: 10 chiefs per lobby

⏳ Match length: \~10 minutes (30 s preparation, 9:30 battle)



🎮 Modes:

Same‑Gen

Only same hero generation

⚠️ Very P2W‑heavy: whales in your generation will dominate many lobbies

Cross‑Gen

All states and generations mixed

✅ Much more F2P‑friendly: power levels are usually more spread out



🗺️ 3) Map \& cities

🔥 Your city can burn down much faster than in normal modes

🔁 When your city falls, you are relocated and continue playing – you are not out

🏛️ There is one central building (Citadel) and 🏰 three side forts (Boulder Forts) that generate Strategic Points



⚔️ 4) Buffs \& combat rules

🚫 Disabled:

President buffs

Dragon buffs

Territory buffs

✅ Active:

Normal 10/20 % stat buffs

Pets/companions function as usual

🩹 Troops you “lose” are not permanently lost:

After the match, troops are healed; 90%+ are counted as wounded only



🎁 5) Why this mode matters

🧪 Strong for pure PvP practice without permanent losses

💎 Solid source of gems, speedups, hero shards, and extra event currency

🏆 With clean play, even F2P can reliably hit 40 000+ points and place top 1–3, especially in Cross‑Gen



🏹 Wild Brawl – Part 2: Points \& Buildings

✅ TL;DR (what to farm)

🏛️ Center (Citadel): biggest income + huge first‑capture spike

🏰 Side forts: stable points per second (great when center is whale‑camped)

🚚 Trucks + 🪂 Airdrops: reliable direct points (especially for F2P)

⏱️ 10‑second capture timer matters: no timer = no income



📌 1) Strategic Points – what gives score?

Wild Brawl uses Strategic Points as the single leaderboard score. You get them mainly from:

🏛️ Center (Citadel) – biggest income

🏰 3 side forts (Boulder Forts) – stable income

🚚 Supply trucks – direct point crates

🪂 Airdrops – direct point crates



🏛️ 2) Center (Citadel)

🧨 First full capture (10‑second control timer) grants a huge one‑time spike (around 7,000+ points)

📈 While you hold the Citadel, you earn continuous points per second

🏆 In many lobbies, whoever gets first‑capture + plays decently stays top 1–3



How to approach center

➡️ Send your strongest main march straight to center in the opening

🔄 If totally outmatched (multiple whales / huge gap), rotate away early instead of suiciding troops



🏰 3) Side forts (Boulder Forts)

❌ No big one‑time bonus like the center

✅ Still give constant points per second while you hold them

👍 Perfect for F2P / mid‑power accounts that can’t reliably hold center

🧠 Great when 1–2 whales camp center



🚚 4) Supply trucks

⏱️ Spawn every \~60 seconds (first around 30 s after battle start)

📍 Appear in three zones (usually marked by circles)

📦 Each truck drops crates that give points when collected



Best practices

🗺️ Learn spawn routes to chain several trucks per rotation

🧯 Perfect fallback when buildings are too hard to hold

✅ For F2P: trucks are your safe, repeatable income



🪂 5) Airdrops

⏱️ Spawn every \~120 seconds (first around 90 s after battle start)

🧭 Spawn in the central region

🕶️ Collect via the Shadow menu: click in time and send a march early enough



Tips

⏳ Watch timer + announcements – send your march before the airdrop lands

🎯 Great for sniping extra points without long building fights



⏱️ 6) Critical rule – 10‑second capture timer

✅ A building counts as captured only after 10 seconds held

❌ Before the timer completes, you get no per‑second income

⚠️ “Bouncing” in/out resets the timer → very inefficient



🩹 7) Troop losses \& risk management

✅ After the match, troops are healed; 90%+ are just wounded

✅ No permanent loss → you can play aggressively

⚠️ In‑match wipes still cost tempo (time refilling instead of scoring)



🚪 8) Early exit = 0 points

❌ Leave before the timer ends → 0 points no matter what

✅ Even a “lost” match can give good rewards if you stay and farm trucks/airdrops smartly



🏹 Wild Brawl – Part 3: Step‑by‑Step Strategy

✅ TL;DR (how to win lobbies)

🏛️ Go for center first capture if you can contest it

🔄 If whales are too strong: hold 🏰 1–2 side forts + farm 🚚/🪂

🚀 Use rockets 1–2 s before impact for max value

🧯 Keep a backup march for refills + endgame snipes



🧭 1) Preparation (0–30 seconds)

👀 Scouting

Check enemy names + power quickly

Mark obvious whales as “avoid” / “play very carefully”

Identify weaker accounts you can realistically contest



🧰 Setup check

Keep at least one backup march with decent power for refills or side pushes



🏛️ 2) First control (from 30 seconds)

🎯 Goal: send main march straight to center (Citadel)

🧨 Reason: first full capture gives a huge one‑time spike and sets up top placements



Practical tips

➡️ Avoid detours – go straight line to center

🔄 If multiple stronger marches arrive earlier, redirect mid‑way to a truck or side fort instead of feeding kills



♟️ 3) March management during battle

🥇 Main march

Focus on building control (center or one key side fort)

Always run it with your best PvP heroes + gear



🔁 Rotation logic

After capturing center:

Keep main march in center

Use backup march for side forts or trucks

If center is lost and not realistically holdable:

Shift to holding 2 side forts and farming points there



🧯 Refills

Keep one march ready to refill garrisons

Refill just before big enemy hits to avoid losing the building



🧨 4) Tactical abilities

🚀 Rockets

Use against big enemy stacks (30 000+ troops) in/near buildings

⏱️ Ideal timing: 1–2 seconds before your own march hits



🧊 Freeze bombs

Best on enemy cities constantly sending troops

They cannot refill or send new marches briefly → window for your push



🩺 Medkits

Use sparingly

Best when heavily injured but you still plan to compete for the rest of the match



🧯 5) Plan B – when you are outmatched

If several players massively outscale you in power, research, and heroes:

🚚 Focus on trucks (routes + crates)

🪂 Focus on airdrops (send early via Shadow menu)

🎯 Opportunistic snipes: when whales are busy at center, take free/weak forts elsewhere



🏁 6) Endgame (last 2–3 minutes)

🛡️ Protect what you already hold; avoid pointless YOLO fights

🔄 Refill buildings to make them hard to flip

🎯 Look for last‑second snipes on free/weak buildings

❌ Don’t waste time on hopeless battles that won’t change the scoreboard

# \# Experts

### 📚 Experts – Introduction – Dawn Academy

#### 📚 Experts – Role \& Overview

• Dawn Academy late-game system — permanent bonuses for city, events, and combat (not playable heroes).

• Unlock at Furnace 25 with Fire Crystal progression (\~Day 150+ on typical servers).

• 9 Experts across 3 generations; each has 4 upgradeable skills + 1 talent (talent scales with affinity).



##### 🔓 Generations \& unlock

• Gen 1 (\~Day 150): Agnes, Cyrille, Holger, Romulus

• Gen 2 (\~Day 195): Baldur, Fabian, Valeria, Ronne

• Gen 3 (\~Day 240): Kathy (eligible states; Frostfire Mine)

• Tundra Trek: 60 encounters → Frontier Trek for directed progress



##### 📈 Progression basics

• Affinity 0–100 via gifts (Compass / Fiery Heart / Sail of Conquest) + Expert Sigils every 10 levels

• Books of Knowledge + learning time for skills (talents auto-scale with affinity)

• Common Sigils → exchange to expert-specific sigils (never blind exchange)



##### 💰 Intimate cost class (not total sigils to max)

• 🟢 50: Agnes, Cyrille · 🟡 60: Baldur · 🟠 80: Holger, Ronne, Kathy

• 🟠 120: Fabian · 🔴 200: Valeria · 💀 360: Romulus

Totals to max differ (e.g. Agnes/Cyrille \~275, Romulus \~1,820 — wostools.net).



##### 🎯 Strengths / ❌ Weaknesses

• Strength: Huge account ROI when you pause at breakpoints (Strategic Pausing).

• Weakness: Resource trap if you max Romulus/Valeria before economy Experts.



##### 💰 F2P vs P2W / 🤓 Tips

• F2P: Agnes → Cyrille → Baldur first; specialists only if you play their mode.

• P4W: Romulus/Valeria/Fabian for combat roles; still keep Baldur for event economy.

### 👩‍💼 Agnes – Gen 1 Expert (Cheap (50))

#### 👩‍💼 Agnes – Role \& Overview

• Gen 1 Expert — city / daily utility (Elite Politician). Best overall F2P Expert.

• Unlocks \~Day 150, Furnace 25. Intimate cost class 50 (cheap).



📈 Key stats (expedition)

• Defense +15% (max talent scaling on expedition)



🎁 How to unlock \& invest

• Tundra Trek story + Dawn Academy; Expert Sigils from Trek, events, packs

• \~275 Expert Sigils to max affinity (community total — wostools.net)

• Recommended stop: Affinity 70, then pivot to Cyrille/Baldur



🛠 Expert skills

1\) Efficient Recon: +8 Intel missions per day

2\) Optimization: +40 Chief Stamina from Storehouse

3\) Project Management: −8h construction time per new build

4\) Covert Knowledge: +120 Mystery Badges/day; +4 Mystery Shop refreshes



⚔️ Talent: Earthbreaker

• 5 Seeker’s Chests per 120 min gathering (max 30/day, UTC reset)



🎯 Strengths / ❌ Weaknesses

• Always-on economy; weak in direct rally combat.

• Mistake: skipping her because she is “not combat”.



💰 F2P vs P2W / 🤓 Tips

• F2P: 10/10 priority. P4W: Still excellent support Expert.

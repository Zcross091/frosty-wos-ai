# \# Heroes



### Rare

📌 Rare Heroes – Overview (Gen 0 / R)



4 Rare Growth heroes from Day 1: Smith, Eugene, Charlie, Cloris. They are not Mythics (Gen 1+) or Epics (Jessie, Bahiti, …).



High level idea (Growth = city + gathering):

• Smith = Iron mine output + iron gathering + infantry AoE

• Eugene = Wood sawmill + wood gathering + infantry spin DPS

• Charlie = Coal mine + coal gathering + lancer AoE/stun

• Cloris = Hunter's Hut + meat gathering + marksman AoE



🎁 Shards

Advanced Recruitment, daily free chests, events. Rare shards are easier than Epic. Typical \~100 shards to max star (lower than Epic/Mythic).



✅ Build order (F2P city focus): Smith → Eugene → Charlie → Cloris (match your resource bottleneck).



💡 Tip: Max Growth skills for economy even if you stop starring combat skills early.



🤝 Combat use: Early exploration only — replace with Epic/Mythic for rallies later.

### Epic

📌 Epic Heroes Overview – Epic Heroes (Gen 0)

📌 Epic Heroes – Overview (Gen 0 / SR)



9 Epic heroes are available from Day 1 alongside Rare starters (Smith, Eugene, etc.). They are not Gen 1 Mythics (Zinman, Molly, …).



High level idea:

• Jessie = Early rally lancer — Trials / Epic Recruitment (F2P #1)

• Bahiti = Exploration single-target marksman DPS

• Sergey = Infantry tank + team damage reduction

• Patrick = Exploration heal + troop HP/ATK buffs

• Gina = AoE marksman + march speed / stamina utility

• Jasser = Marksman DPS + research speed

• Seo-yoon = Team ATK/speed buffer + healing speed

• Ling Xue = Lancer AoE + training speed

• Lumak Bokan = Expedition debuff + hunt march speed



🎁 Shards

Epic Recruitment, Advanced Recruitment, Polar Terror / Intel (epic missions), Epic General Hero Shards. Target 600 shards for max star.



✅ Build order (general): Jessie → Bahiti or Sergey → Patrick / Gina → others by shards.



⚠️ Auto-join rallies: Use a blue hero in slot 1 if others bring Jessie — avoid stealing a max-skill Jessie slot.



🤝 Early march examples: Sergey + Jessie + Bahiti | Sergey + Jessie + Patrick

### Gen 1

📌 Gen 1 Overview – Generation 1

📌 Generation 1 – Hero Comparison (Zinman / Molly / Natalia / Jeronimo)



Gen 1 unlocks at Day 0 (start of server). Gen 2 follows around Day 40+ (Alonso / Flint / Philly).



High level idea:

• Zinman = Marksman / Growth — building speed \& resource savings (F2P Lucky Wheel hero)

• Molly = Lancer DPS + rally buffers — free from Day-2 sign-in

• Natalia = Infantry control / beast damage — paywall + limited F2P Foundry shards

• Jeronimo = Infantry rally DPS — VIP / Hall of Chief only



🔨 Zinman – Marksman / Growth

Pros: Bastionist cuts basic resource cost 3–15% and speeds building 3–15%; strong early arena backline; Positional Batter +5–25% troop damage.

Cons: One combat expedition buff; many F2P skip wheel for Gen 2 Flint; 4★ enough for construction perks (per community guides).

Best for: Builders, early progression, optional Lucky Wheel investment.



❄️ Molly – Lancer / Combat

Pros: Free shards (7-day sign-in Day 2, recruitment, HoH); Stand of Arms +5–25% rally damage; Bulwarks −5–20% damage taken.

Cons: Outscaled by later-gen Lancers; exploration stats lower than mythic Gen 2+.

Best for: Early rally joiner / F2P damage before Gen 3 Mia.



🐻 Natalia – Infantry / Combat

Pros: Stun chance (Wildling Roar up to 20%); beast rally damage +5–25%; pet training at Furnace Lv18+.

Cons: Any purchase to unlock; Natalia shards not in General Mythic shards; Foundry only during Gen 1 (3 shards/battle cap).

Best for: Low spenders who buy first pack; beast / bear focus.



⚔️ Jeronimo – Infantry / Combat

Pros: Rally damage +5–25% (Battle Manifesto); troop Attack +5–25%; stun chance on attacks; Natural Leader talent at max star: +15% Lethality \& Health to all deployed troops.

Cons: Pay-only (VIP / Hall of Chief); cannot use General Mythic shards on exclusive heroes.

Best for: Spenders wanting early rally leader power.



🎁 Shard sources (overview)

• Zinman: Lucky Wheel (Gen 1 banner), Hall of Heroes, Arena Shop, Foundry, Daily Deals.

• Molly: 7-day sign-in, Hero Recruitment, Lighthouse Intel, Molly's Blessing, HoH.

• Natalia / Jeronimo: VIP packs, Foundry (Gen 1 only for Natalia), Hall of Chief (Jeronimo).



⭐ Shard roadmap: Mythic heroes use 600 shards to max star (10/40/115/300/600 per tier). F2P: prioritize Molly + optional Zinman 4★; skip pay heroes unless you spend.



✅ Recommended build order

1\) F2P: Molly → save gems for Gen 2 Flint (skip or 4★ Zinman for buildings only)

2\) Low spend: Molly + Natalia if you bought first pack

3\) P2W: Jeronimo + Natalia for early rallies; still get Molly free



🤝 Early march ideas: Molly rally lead or join; Zinman backline exploration; Natalia beast rallies.



Sources: whiteoutdata.com, whiteoutsurvival.wiki (Gen 1 \~Day 0, Gen 2 \~Day 40).

📌 Gen 16 Overview – Generation 16

📌 Generation 16 – Hero Comparison (Seigel / Ursar / Aisling)



Quick overview of the three Generation 16 heroes. Gen 16 unlocks when your State/Server is around 1160+ days old (\~80 days after Gen 15). Gen 17 follows later on the same \~80-day hero cycle.



High level idea:

• Seigel = Infantry wall, reflect, garrison and rally defense

• Ursar = Lancer support, HoH exclusive, poison and lethality buffs

• Aisling = Marksman AoE/control, best F2P DPS via Lucky Wheel



🛡️ Seigel – Infantry Tank \& Defender

Pros: Massive Health, Spike Guard reflect, Cursed Protection opening immunity, Hell's Vow garrison lethality.

Cons: Lower Attack; build after DPS unless you urgently need defense.

Best for: Garrison leads, rally defense, players who want an unbreakable frontline.



🐻 Ursar – Lancer Support \& Rally Buff

Pros: HoH hero for Gen 16; Horn of the Ancients and Poison Tips for rallies; Typhoon Drums rally Attack.

Cons: HoH Marks do not help Aisling/Seigel — only Ursar from that shop.

Best for: Rally support, Lancer/Marksman lethality stacks, mid-game power spike with HoH.



🌲 Aisling – Marksman DPS \& Control

Pros: Highest Attack; Wild Growth AoE stun; expedition Marksman damage (Rock Storm, Forest Fury); team heal.

Cons: Squishier than Seigel; shard farm via Lucky Wheel when Gen 16 is live.

Best for: Main F2P DPS priority; expedition and rally damage.



🎁 Shard sources (all three)

HoH (Ursar only), Lucky Wheel (\~115 milestone shards per event), King of Icefield / SVS, Foundry, Daily Deals, Hero Rally, Mythic General shards. 600 shards each to max star.



✅ Recommended build order

1\) F2P: Aisling → Ursar → Seigel

2\) P2W: Aisling → Ursar → Seigel (or Seigel first if pure garrison/SVS defense)



🤝 Standard march: Seigel + Ursar + Aisling



Adjust for your shard luck and spend level; Gen 16 continues the Infantry / Lancer / Marksman triangle with defense, support, and ranged burst.
